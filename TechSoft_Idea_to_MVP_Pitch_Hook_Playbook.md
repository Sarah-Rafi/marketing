# TechSoft — Idea to MVP: Pitch & Hook Playbook

Prepared for TechSoft | Platforms: LinkedIn, Instagram, X | As of November 12, 2026

## 1. Positioning: What We Are Selling

TechSoft turns three things into working software: a problem someone wants fixed, a manual process someone wants automated, and an idea someone wants to bring to life. Every post, Reel and thread in this playbook points to one action: tell us what you need, and we build the MVP together.

> Important: everything in [brackets] must be replaced with true TechSoft facts (project names, numbers, timelines, prices, client permission). Never invent results, clients or numbers. This playbook builds on the Social Media Launch Plan, the Health, Fitness and Wellness Playbook and the AI Impact Report.

### Core Message (Use Everywhere)

> "Got an idea, a problem, or a process that eats your time? Tell us. We'll build it with you, starting with an MVP."

### Short Versions

| Use | Line |
| --- | --- |
| Headline | Your idea, built. |
| One-liner | From idea to MVP, with a team that has shipped [X] websites and apps. |
| Problem angle | If it's slowing your business down, we can build the fix. |
| Automation angle | If you do it every day by hand, it can probably be automated. |
| Founder angle | You bring the idea. We bring the team. Let's build the MVP. |

### Why This Angle Works Now

- The AI Impact Report shows building custom software is cheaper than before, so more businesses now consider building instead of buying.
- Many founders try no-code or vibe coding first and get stuck. They still need a team for reliability, security, scale and maintenance.
- An MVP is a low-risk first step. It lowers the buyer's fear of a big budget and a long project.

## 2. The Offer: Three Doors In, One Way Forward

Customers arrive through one of three doors, and all three lead to the same offer: a free call, a clear scope, then an MVP. Every hook should open one door clearly, so the viewer thinks "that's me" in the first 3 seconds. The door is only for the hook: do not answer each need with its own call to action. Every piece ends with the same general message (see section 7).

| Door | What the customer is thinking | What we build |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Fix a problem | "Customers can't book easily" / "Our website loses sales" / "Our app keeps crashing" | Rebuilt website, booking system, customer portal, app rescue |
| 2. Automate the business | "We run everything on WhatsApp, Excel and paper" / "My team wastes hours on the same task" | Internal dashboards, order and inventory systems, CRM, AI assistants, integrations |
| 3. Bring an idea to life | "I've had this app idea for years but I'm not technical" | MVP mobile app or web app, clickable prototype, launch-ready product |

### What "Let's Build Your MVP" Means (Explain It Simply)

Most buyers do not know the word MVP. Say it in plain words: "the smallest version of your idea that real users can try."

1. Free idea call ([30] minutes): you tell us the idea, the problem, or the process.
2. Scope: we pick the few features that matter most and cut the rest.
3. Design: you see clickable screens before any code is written.
4. Build: we build the MVP in [X] weeks, with demos along the way.
5. Launch and learn: real users try it; we improve it based on what they do.

### Promises We Can Make (Only If True)

- [Fixed scope and price before we start]
- [You see progress every week]
- [You own the code and the design]
- [We help after launch: fixes, updates, scaling]

Do not promise a timeline or price in posts unless the team has confirmed it.

### Hook Rules

- Say it in under 12 words; the first 3 seconds decide everything.
- Name the person ("Gym owners", "Not technical?") or the pain ("Excel", "WhatsApp").

- The hook opens one door; the ending is always the same general message, never a door-specific CTA.
- Proof hooks use real numbers only; leave [brackets] until you have them.

## 5. Reel and Short Video Scripts

These 8 scripts run 20 to 45 seconds each and work as Instagram Reels, LinkedIn native video and X video. Shoot face to camera or screen recording; add the hook as on-screen text in the first frame.

### Script 1: "From Notes App to App Store" (Idea door)

- Hook (0 to 3s): "That app idea sitting in your Notes app? Watch this."
- Shot 1: Phone screen, a messy note: "App idea: booking for [type of business]"
- Shot 2: Hand sketch on paper of 3 screens
- Shot 3: Same screens in Figma
- Shot 4: Working app on a real phone [from an approved project]
- On-screen text: "Idea, Sketch, Design, MVP"
- CTA: "Whatever you need, it starts with a conversation. Let's talk."

### Script 2: "Give Us Your Idea in One Sentence" (Recurring series)

- Hook: "You gave us your app idea in one sentence. Here's how we'd build it."
- Body: Read a follower's comment on screen. Team member sketches the MVP on a whiteboard: 3 core features, 1 thing to skip, rough steps.
- Close: "This is the smallest version that real users can try."
- CTA: "Whatever you need, it starts with a conversation. Let's talk." (In the caption, invite viewers to comment their idea for next week's pick.)
- Why it works: viewers comment to get featured, which boosts reach and starts real conversations.

### Script 3: "Stop Doing This by Hand" (Automate door)

- Hook: "If your business runs on this, we need to talk." (show a messy spreadsheet and 40 WhatsApp chats)
- Body: Split screen. Left: the manual way (copy, paste, call, chase). Right: one dashboard that does it [approved project screens or a mock-up].
- Line: "Same business. [X] fewer hours a week." [real number only]
- CTA: "Whatever you need, it starts with a conversation. Let's talk."

### Script 4: "What Goes in Your MVP" (MVP explainer)

- Hook: "Your app doesn't need 40 features. It needs 3."
- Body: Whiteboard list of 10 features for an example app. Cross out 7 one by one: chat, AI everything, social feed, dark mode...
- Keep: sign-up, the core action, payments.
- Line: "Launch this. Learn. Then add the rest."
- CTA: "Whatever you need, it starts with a conversation. Let's talk."

### Script 5: "POV: You Finally Built It" (Emotional, trend format)

- Hook (text): "POV: you stopped saying 'someday' about your app idea."
- Body: Quick cuts with trending audio. Founder on a call with the team, screens appearing, test on phone, first user sign-up notification.
- End card: "Let's bring your idea to life. TechSoft."
- CTA in caption: "Whatever you need, it starts with a conversation. Let's talk."

### Script 6: "Built It With AI and It Broke" (Fix door)

- Hook: "AI built your app in a weekend. Now it's breaking. Here's why."
- Point 1: The demo works, but real users break it.
- Point 2: No security review; customer data is at risk.
- Point 3: Nobody knows how to fix or scale it.
- Line: "AI is great for testing an idea. A team makes it last."
- CTA: "Whatever you need, it starts with a conversation. Let's talk."
- Note: This fits the AI Impact Report advice to sell reliability, security and maintenance.

### Script 7: "Before and After" (Proof)

- Hook: "This is how [client type] took bookings before. Now swipe."
- Body: Old flow (phone calls, paper diary) then the new app flow in 3 taps [approved screens].
- Line: "[Real result]."
- CTA: "Whatever you need, it starts with a conversation. Let's talk."

### Script 8: "Meet the Team Building Your App" (Trust)

- Hook: "Here's who builds your app when you work with TechSoft."
- Body: 3 to 5 team members, 3 seconds each: name, role, one line ("I make sure your app doesn't crash").
- CTA: "Whatever you need, it starts with a conversation. Let's talk."

## 9. Turning Attention Into Projects

A hook earns a view; this flow turns the view into a call. Every post should push people one step down it.

### Lead Flow

1. Hook stops the scroll (Section 4).
2. Viewer comments a keyword ("IDEA", "MVP") or answers a question.
3. We reply within 24 hours and send the free resource by DM.
4. DM follow-up asks one question about their idea or problem.
5. Offer a free [30]-minute idea call.
6. On the call: understand the idea, suggest the MVP scope, send a short proposal.
