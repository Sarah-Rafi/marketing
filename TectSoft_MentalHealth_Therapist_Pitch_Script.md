## Research behind it (for sales conversations; none of these numbers are in the video)

- **Booked out:** in the APA 2024 Practitioner Pulse Survey, 53% of US psychologists had no openings for new patients (56% in 2023, about 60% in 2022). This was a small sample (853 responses, about a 3% response rate). [APA 2024](https://www.apa.org/pubs/reports/practitioner/2024), [APA 2023](https://apa.org/pubs/reports/practitioner/2023-psychologist-reach-limits)
- **Tracking progress between sessions helps:** routine outcome monitoring has a small but consistent effect on outcomes. It is larger for patients who are going off track: feedback roughly doubled reliable change for clients predicted to do poorly. [Lambert et al. 2018](https://digitalcommons.usu.edu/psych_facpub/1871), [Barkham et al. 2023](https://eprints.whiterose.ac.uk/197689/), [umbrella review](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11703940)
- **Homework matters:** in CBT, patients who complete more of their between-session homework have better outcomes (Kazantzis et al. 2016, 17 studies, 2,312 clients). This is why an app that tracks completion of assigned exercises is useful. [Kazantzis 2016](https://research.bond.edu.au/en/publications/quantity-and-quality-of-homework-compliance-a-meta-analysis-of-re/)
- **AI has to sit under the clinician:** Illinois' Wellness and Oversight for Psychological Resources Act (Aug 2025) bans AI therapy that lacks licensed-professional oversight. Nevada passed a similar restriction, and Utah requires disclosure. The pitch therefore always shows the AI following the therapist's approved steps, sending warning signs to the therapist, and keeping a crisis line on screen. [Becker's](https://www.beckershospitalreview.com/?p=303810), [Baker Botts](https://ourtake.bakerbotts.com/post/102lr2p/illinois-enacts-first-in-the-nation-guardrails-on-ai-therapy)

## What TectSoft would build (talking points)
1. Branded patient app: daily check-ins (mood, sleep, anxiety, or validated scales such as PHQ-9 and GAD-7 if the clinic wants them), journal, assigned exercises, reminders.
2. Clinician dashboard: progress between sessions, homework adherence, trends, pre-session summaries.
3. AI between sessions, limited to the clinician's approved content: guided breathing, grounding and CBT exercises at 4 a.m. Warning signs go to the clinician and a crisis line is always on screen.
4. Waitlist onboarding: psychoeducation and self-guided basics until a slot opens.
5. Privacy and compliance by design: HIPAA, GDPR or local rules, consent, audit logs.
