# TechSoft MVP Trailer: Production Brief (30 sec)

Format: 16:9 landscape (export a 9:16 crop for Reels). Tone: cinematic, warm, trustworthy. Original music only, with no franchise music, logos, characters or look-alikes. No promises of success.

Captions: `TechSoft_MVP_Trailer_Captions.srt` (same folder), timed to the voiceover below.

## Voiceover (read exactly as written)

> You've been thinking about it for a while. A problem worth solving. An idea that could make a difference. But what should you build first? Start with a conversation. We'll help you explore the problem, shape your idea, and plan an MVP—an app or website built around what people need. Your next big idea starts with one conversation. Let's talk.

Voice direction: natural, confident, unhurried (about 2.3 words a second). Pause about 0.5 s after "first?" and about 1 s before "Let's talk."

## Shot Prompts (paste each into your video generator)

**Shot 1 (0–5 s): Alone with the idea**
Cinematic night interior, a lone founder in their 30s at a desk in a quiet apartment, lit only by a warm desk lamp and the cool glow of a city window. They study a rough pencil sketch of app screens on paper. Slow push-in, shallow depth of field, soft film grain, teal-and-amber grade, contemplative mood.

**Shot 2 (5–10 s): Fragments of the idea**
Fast cinematic macro close-ups cut together: handwritten notes with arrows and circled words; a phone lighting up with a simple wireframe; fingers tapping a basic clickable prototype on a laptop. Warm practical light, shallow focus, subtle camera motion, rising energy.

**Shot 3 (10–20 s): The conversation**
Bright, welcoming modern studio office in daylight. The founder sits at a table with two friendly developers, a diverse team in casual clothes. They talk, laugh, sketch user flows on a whiteboard, and review an app on a phone and a website on a large monitor together. Handheld documentary feel, warm natural light, genuine collaboration.

**Shot 4 (20–27 s): It's real**
The founder alone again, now in soft morning light, confidently swiping through a polished mobile app prototype and a matching website on a laptop. A small, quiet smile. Slow orbit camera move, golden-hour rim light, hopeful and resolved.

**Shot 5 (27–30 s): End card**
Clean dark background with a subtle warm light sweep. Text, centered: "Your next big idea starts with a conversation." Below it, larger: "Let's talk." Add the TechSoft logo and handle.

## Music Brief

Original cinematic instrumental, 30 seconds. It opens with sparse, atmospheric piano and soft synth pads (0–5 s), adds pulsing strings and light percussion that build steadily (5–20 s), rises to a warm, hopeful swell with full strings (20–27 s), then resolves on one sustained chord into the end card. Emotional and uplifting, not aggressive.

## Edit Notes

- Duck the music about 6 dB under the voiceover.
- Use cuts in Shot 2; use soft dissolves into Shot 3 and into the end card.
- Captions go in the bottom third: white text with a soft dark shadow, at most 2 lines.
